# 📊 Executive Business Reporting (EBR) Dashboard

## Excel-Based Business Intelligence & Executive Reporting Solution

An interactive **Executive Business Reporting (EBR)** solution built entirely in **Microsoft Excel** to transform structured retail data into executive-level insights across revenue, profitability, customers, products, and operations.

The project was developed for a fictional consumer-electronics retailer, **Apex Retail Group**, operating across **North America, Europe, and Asia**.

The objective was to create a reporting system that allows decision-makers to quickly understand business performance, identify trends, and investigate commercial and operational drivers.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Apex Retail Group operates across multiple markets, stores, product categories, and customer segments.

The EBR solution was designed to answer key management questions such as:

- How is overall revenue performing?
- Which markets contribute the most revenue?
- Which product categories generate the strongest results?
- Where are profitability opportunities and weaknesses?
- How many customers and orders are being served?
- Which areas of the business require further investigation?
- How has performance changed over time?

The final solution converts these questions into an interactive executive reporting environment.

## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Features

The solution was built entirely in Microsoft Excel using:

- **Power Query** (Data preparation and cleaning)
- **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts** (Aggregation and visual reporting)
- **Slicers & Timeline Filters** (Dynamic filtering across dimensions)
- **Form Controls** (Interactive navigation)
- **Camera Tool** (Dynamic dashboard layout design)
- **Conditional Formatting** (Visual performance indicators)
- **Dynamic Named Ranges & Excel Formulas** (Flexible calculations)
- **Data Modelling Techniques** (Star schema implementation)

> No external BI platform was required for the final reporting solution.

## 📊 Executive Reporting Structure

The EBR solution contains three major reporting areas:

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of core KPIs to give management an immediate understanding of overall business health:
- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Orders
- Customers
- Units Sold

### 2. Commercial Dashboard
Focuses on revenue and profitability performance to identify top commercial drivers across:
- Markets & Product categories
- Revenue vs. Profit vs. Margin
- Historical sales trends

### 3. Customer & Operations Dashboard
Delivers operational visibility beyond financial metrics by tracking:
- Customer activity & related metrics
- Order performance & Units sold
- Store performance & Operational trends

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

- 🇺🇸 **United States:** Generated the highest revenue among the markets analysed, representing the largest contributor to commercial performance.
- 💻 **Computers:** Emerged as the strongest-performing product category based on revenue performance.
- 🏠 **Home Appliances:** Showed significant profitability, highlighting that high-revenue categories do not always equal the strongest profit generators.
- 🎮 **Games & Toys:** Recorded the lowest revenue among major product categories, signaling an area for potential demand, pricing, or product mix optimization.
- 📉 **Revenue Trend:** Revenue peaked in 2019 before declining in 2020, offering a critical signal for management evaluating historical performance shifts.

## 📈 Reporting Philosophy

The EBR was designed around a core principle:

> **Turn business activity into measurable performance, then turn performance into decisions.**

```text
Business Data ──> Data Preparation ──> Structured Analysis ──> KPIs & Metrics ──> Interactive Reporting ──> Business Insights
```
---

## ⚙️ Data Preparation Workflow

The reporting workflow consisted of several sequential stages:

1. **Data Import:** Source datasets were imported into Excel using Power Query.
2. **Data Preparation:** Data was cleaned and structured for analysis, including sales records, customer information, product information, store information, and exchange-rate data.
3. **Data Modelling:** Datasets were organised into a structured analytical model centred around the `Sales` fact table.
4. **KPI Development:** Core business metrics were defined to provide consistent performance reporting.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and other Excel functionality were used to create the reporting interface.
6. **Executive Reporting:** Final dashboards were designed to allow users to move seamlessly from high-level performance to deeper commercial and operational analysis.
